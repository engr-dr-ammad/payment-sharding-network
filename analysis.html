Understanding the Trade-off:
  • Optimistic: Maximum throughput (50-100 tx/s), fast settlement, requires challenge-period security
  • Probabilistic: Balanced approach (20-40 tx/s), medium finality after k blocks, suitable for most payments
  • Deterministic: Absolute finality (<20 tx/s), highest security, ideal for high-value settlement

🇪🇺 Digital Euro Design Implications

Key Findings for Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC):
  • Privacy-First Design: ZK proofs enable optional privacy for citizens while maintaining regulatory oversight through transaction monitoring
  • Scalability: Sharded architecture supports 50-100 tx/s per shard, enabling continental-scale settlement
  • Policy Flexibility: Finalization level can be selected per transaction class (retail vs wholesale)
  • Interoperability: Cross-shard transactions queued for probabilistic settlement
